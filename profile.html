Abhinav Sharma

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Transportation Engineering at North Carolina State University. My research focuses on understanding the behavioral dynamics and safety implications of automated vehicles.

Specifically, it includes designing controlled experiments, analyzing real-world driving data, developing evaluation methods, and identifying safety-critical scenarios for automated vehicles. This work supports the development of safer automated driving systems and contributes to improved public road safety.
