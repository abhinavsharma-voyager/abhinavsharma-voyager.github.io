I am a Ph.D. candidate in Transportation Engineering at North Carolina State University. My research focuses on understanding the behavioral dynamics and safety implications of automated vehicles.

Specifically, it includes identifying safety-critical scenarios, designing controlled experiments, analyzing real-world driving data, and proposing new testing and evaluation techniques for automated vehicles. This work supports the development of safer autonomous systems and contributes to improved public road safety.
