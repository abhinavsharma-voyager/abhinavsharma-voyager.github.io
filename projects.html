Academic Projects

  1. 2024

    Graduate Project

    Performance Evaluation of a Freeway Segment Using TransModeler 7.0

    North Carolina State University

    • Evaluated a 5-mile, 5-interchange segment of the I-264 freeway in Portsmouth, Virginia, using TransModeler microsimulation, analyzing traffic demand, headways, speeds, travel times, and lane-changing behavior.
    • Identified merge and diverge zones, particularly the L4–L6 segment, as critical bottlenecks driven by heavy lane changing combined with high on-ramp merging volumes.
    • Found that peak-hour congestion builds rapidly between 6:30 and 8:00 AM, with downstream speeds dropping to 10–30 mph and travel times rising from 50–60 seconds to 70–90 seconds.
    • Estimated congestion growth of 0.055 min/mile per minute (about 3.3 seconds of added delay per mile each minute), providing baseline metrics for designing and evaluating mitigation strategies.
    • TransModeler
    • Traffic Microsimulation
    • Freeway Operations
    • Bottleneck Analysis
    • Congestion Analysis
